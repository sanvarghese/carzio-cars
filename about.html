About CARZIO CARS

We aim to be one of the reputed pre-owned vehicle dealers in New Zealand. Autonest strives for the best quality and reliable pre-owned cars to our valued customers. We offer a great selection of multi brand car models, including hatchback, sedan coupé, wagon, SUV and other high performance models. Before we list any car at Autonest we will ensure that it is in perfect condition and customers enjoy the most satisfying driving experience.

The way we do business is pretty straightforward.

You know what you need. So when you visit our car yard, we’ll let you take the lead. Take a test drive. Every vehicle you see has a low, no-haggle price, so you can focus on what matters. Of course, our friendly sales consultants will always be there if you have any questions.
