Privacy Act 2020 (NZ) Declaration

Carzio Cars is collecting financial and personal information about you which will be used by third party lenders (the Finance Company/ies) to determine whether to open a credit account in your name. In assessing whether to open a credit account in your name, information collected and held about you may be exchanged with organisations including other related companies, credit reporting agencies, financial institutions, and government agencies. The information may be used by the Finance Company/ies for credit assessment and control purposes and to register security interests. The information obtained, exchanged, and held may include, but is not limited to, Driver Licence Checks, Fines Checks, Credit Checks, and Police Checks.

Under the Privacy Act 2020 (NZ) (Privacy Act), you have rights to access and correct personal information. You may also request full details of every organisation or person to whom Carzio Cars and the Finance Company/ies has disclosed information about you.

The Finance Company/ies may exchange information about you with credit reporting agencies and Government Departments on an ongoing basis for purposes relating to the provision of credit account to you. The credit reporting agencies and Government Departments will hold that information about you on their systems and use it to provide their credit reporting and other services.

By lodging an application with Carzio Cars you consent to:

  • Carzio Cars sending this information to the Finance Company/ies and
  • Carzio Cars and the Finance Company/ies carrying out electronic ID verification using biometric checks, global watchlists and Australian and New Zealand data sources to obtain information about you, including but not limited to identification data (i.e. name, address, telephone number, email address), profile information (i.e. age, gender), and electronic identification data (i.e. IP addresses, MACIDs, cookies)

The information we collect may be used for communicating with you electronically, this may include promotional and other materials about the products and services, and generally to do business with you.

In order to assess your loan application, Carzio Cars and the Finance Company/ies may need to verify certain information from another person or entity who is not an applicant on the loan. This Privacy Declaration outlines the reason why we collect certain information, who we share it with, and how we use that information. You should ensure that you have permission to use and provide this information. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy.

You understand and agree to the terms and conditions provided above. You also confirm that all the information you provide is accurate, up to date, complete, and relevant. You also confirm that information about you provided to Carzio Cars may be disclosed to the Finance Company/ies, including their related companies, agents, associates, and transferees.

You also consent to:

  • Being sent promotional and other materials
  • ID checks
  • Driver checks

  • Credit checks
  • Police checks
