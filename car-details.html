Vehicle Description

Introducing the stunning 2024 Toyota Supra - a perfect blend of performance, style, and technology. This sports coupe has been meticulously maintained with only 1,270 km on the odometer, making it practically brand new.

The Supra features a powerful 2.0L turbocharged engine paired with a smooth manual transmission, delivering exhilarating performance while maintaining excellent fuel efficiency. The exterior sports a sleek Pearl White finish that turns heads wherever you go, complemented by premium alloy wheels and LED lighting.

Inside, you'll find a luxurious cabin with leather seats, dual-zone climate control, and state-of-the-art technology including Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, and a premium audio system. Safety is paramount with 5-star ANCAP rating, featuring advanced driver assistance systems including adaptive cruise control, lane keep assist, and blind spot monitoring.

This vehicle comes with full service history and balance of factory warranty. Perfect for enthusiasts seeking a thrilling driving experience without compromising on comfort and practicality.
